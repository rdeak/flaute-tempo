# Privacy Policy for Flute Assistant

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Flute Assistant (the "app") is a metronome and chromatic tuner for Android, published by
bertlab. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

## Summary

The app does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal data. It has no account
system, no analytics, no advertising and does not use the internet.

## Microphone

The tuner feature uses the microphone to detect the pitch of the note you are playing. Audio
is analysed in real time in the device's memory only. It is never recorded, saved to storage,
or sent anywhere. The microphone is active only while the Tuner tab is open and the app is on
screen, and you can revoke the permission at any time in Android Settings. The metronome does
not use the microphone.

## Data stored on your device

Your metronome settings (tempo, meter, subdivision, sound, volume), tuner reference pitch and
saved presets are stored locally in the app's private storage so they are there the next time
you open the app. They never leave the device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If you use
Android's device backup, this data may be included in your own backup managed by Google or
your device maker, under their terms.

## Notifications

While the metronome is running in the background the app shows a notification with the
current tempo and a Stop button. This requires the notification permission on Android 13 and
later. No notification content is shared with anyone.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from
anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with an updated
